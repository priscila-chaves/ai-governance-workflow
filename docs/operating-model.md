# Operating Model

## Structure

### Business Units
- Own AI systems
- Perform risk assessments
- Provide documentation

### AI CoE
- Defines governance framework
- Validates risk assessments
- Performs conformity assessment
- Approves AI systems

### AI Ethics & Compliance Committee
- Provides legal interpretation
- Reviews high-risk AI systems
- Ensures EU AI Act compliance

---

## Design Principles

- Federated execution, central governance
- Risk-based tiering
- Minimal involvement of Legal unless required
- Automation-first approach
